the ecosystem

One day's work,
and who does
which part of it.

Read down: this is the order the work actually happens in. Five of the 7 products show up at more than one stage — and none of them needs the others to do its own part.

  1. 1

    Capture

    Something happens, and it goes on the record instead of into somebody’s memory.

    • Mimic runs the interview
    • Recapr records the meeting
    • lexerai takes in the document
  2. 2

    Understand

    The record is read, and comes back as something you can act on.

    • Mimic scores each answer
    • Recapr writes the summary
    • lexerai pulls out the fields
  3. 3

    Organise

    It stops being an event and becomes a record somebody owns.

    • NousCRM opportunity, customer, invoice
    • Task Manager planned onto a board
  4. 4

    Act

    The work is assigned, done, approved and closed.

    • Task Manager execute and close
    • NousCRM approvals, and what is owed

  5. One engine underneath

    Every one of those four asks the same engine to do the thinking. That is the whole idea.

    • AI Engine the same engine, every time

AI Engine 06

One engine.
Every product
metered through it.

One engine serves the whole suite: it routes each request between a hosted frontier model and a local small model, scrubs PII before anything reaches a model, and answers from a knowledge base you load. Every product is metered through it.

what the product sends

Raise an invoice for Nurul Hakim at nurul@example.com — he called from +60 12-345 6789 about the September renewal.

redacted before the model

Raise an invoice for [NAME] at [EMAIL] — he called from [PHONE] about the September renewal.

Sample request. The PII scrubbing it shows is on by default.

gemini-2.5-flashhosted frontier modelRouted to Gemini
qwen3-4blocal small modelSLM acceptable

Shadow-comparing the two routes is on the roadmap, not shipped. The console says so itself: the comparison pipeline is not built yet. So it is not claimed here.

Model routing
A hosted frontier model and a local small model, with the console recording which one served each request.
PII scrubbing
On by default. Personal data is redacted before the model ever sees it.
Knowledge base
Load PDF, DOCX or TXT; the console reports documents indexed, total chunks and embeddings.
Observability
Requests, latency, tokens and errors — by product and by task, each with a request ID.
