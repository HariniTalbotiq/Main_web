Nine ways to interview a candidate, and the rest of the suite.

TalbotIQ makes nine interview formats, the recruitment software that turns a scorecard into a pipeline stage, and six business tools: a sales CRM, a business management system (ERP) for SMEs, a task and productivity manager, document management, a meeting note taker, and the private AI engine every product runs on.

The interview formats run on Mimic, the interview platform: you define one rubric, candidates take their round in a browser or on the phone they already have, and the platform recommends. It never rejects anyone; a named person makes every decision.

  • Nine interview formats, compared below: live or asynchronous, who asks the questions, how answers are scored, and whether a camera is needed.
  • One rubric across the scored formats, with each score linked to the transcript or moment it came from, so a first-round score and a final-round score sit on the same axis.
  • Nobody is rejected by a model. The platform does the arithmetic; the shortlist is a recommendation and a named person takes every decision.
  • One private AI engine under every product, scrubbing personal data before anything reaches a model.
  • Pricing is quote-based. There is no public price list; book a demo and we quote for your volume.

Frequently asked questions

Can I combine several interview formats in one hiring process?+
Yes. Mimic runs interview pipelines, so a role can chain rounds in different formats, with candidates advanced by drag or by score threshold, and every scored round uses the same rubric, so scores from different rounds are comparable.
Does the AI decide who gets hired?+
No. The platform scores answers against the rubric you defined and links each score to the evidence it came from, but it only recommends. Mimic never rejects anyone, and in the recruitment software advancing a candidate, approving a requisition and signing an offer are actions a named person takes.
Is TalbotIQ an applicant tracking system (ATS)?+
The Intelligent Recruitment Software is the ATS: seven modules and one administration area on one licence and one database, with a Hire Mode for internal talent teams and an Agency Mode for staffing firms that bill clients. The interview formats feed it, but each can also be used on its own.
How much does TalbotIQ cost?+
Pricing is quote-based and depends on volume and the products you take; there is no public price list. Book a demo and we quote for your team.
Where does candidate and customer data go?+
Every product runs on the same private AI engine, which scrubs personal data before any request reaches a model and records which model served each request. The Vawlt page lists, task by task, which run on our own hardware and which do not.
Which interview formats work without a camera?+
Voice Interview and Chat Interview do not need a camera; Video Interview, Recorded Video Interview and Conversational AI Avatar Interview do. Each product page states what a candidate needs before the round.
